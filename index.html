Mighty Hammer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Mighty Hammer is a single-player hammer-throw target game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What Mighty Hammer stores

Your level progress (stars, best scores, coins, daily reward) and app settings (music, sound effects, haptics) are saved only on your device. Mighty Hammer has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own — we never see this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising (version 1.1 and later)

From version 1.1, Mighty Hammer is free and supported by ads shown through Google AdMob on Android and iOS. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID or IDFA), IP address, coarse location derived from it, and coarse ad-interaction data, as described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data disclosure. This data goes to Google, not to us.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA and the UK), the game asks for your consent before any ad data is processed, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the game. On iOS, the game also asks for permission through App Tracking Transparency; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised.

The game itself plays offline. The only network traffic is the ad SDK. Version 1.0 (Android closed test) contained no ads and did not access the internet.

What Mighty Hammer does not do

The app has no analytics of its own, requests no access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location, and does not sell data.

Children

Mighty Hammer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated at this address with a new date before that version is released.
