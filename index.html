Mighty Hammer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Mighty Hammer is an offline hammer-throw target game. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

What Mighty Hammer stores

Your level progress (stars, best scores, coins) and app settings (music, sound effects, haptics) are saved only on your device. Mighty Hammer has no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server of its own — we never see your data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What Mighty Hammer does not do

The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal or device data. It requests no permissions, contains no advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not access the internet.

Children

Mighty Hammer does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, advertising), this policy will be updated at this address with a new date before that version is released.
